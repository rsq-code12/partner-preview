Who we are

Alurei is a family care app, operated by The Seasons (FZE), Block C, Sharjah Free Zone, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates (“Alurei”, “we”, “us”). We are the controller of the partner information described here.

Who this policy covers

It covers:

  • Activity partners: classes, play centres, camps and other venues listed in families’ Daily Flow
  • Reward partners: businesses that welcome Alurei nannies and families, and brands that offer them perks
  • Agency and referral partners: nanny agencies, clinics and others who refer families to Alurei
  • Businesses we contact about becoming a partner

It applies to the people who act for these businesses: owners, managers, staff and branch teams. Information about the business itself, like its trade name or address, is mostly not personal data, but we treat it with the same care.

Families and nannies who use the Alurei app are covered by the Alurei app Privacy Policy.

What families and nannies see

Your public listing is shown to Alurei families and nannies. That includes your business name, logo, photos, address, opening hours, classes, offers and booking link or WhatsApp number. Staff names and personal contact details are never shown, unless you choose to put them in your listing.

Who else we share it with

We only share partner information when we need to:

  • Service providers who run Alurei for us, under contract and only on our instructions: cloud hosting, databases and file storage, email and messaging (including SMS and WhatsApp), analytics, customer support tools, automation tools and payment providers
  • Gift card and voucher providers, to deliver the vouchers and rewards used at partner businesses
  • Professional advisers, such as accountants, auditors and lawyers
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of Alurei, our users or others
  • A buyer or investor, if Alurei is reorganised, merged or sold, under confidentiality

We will tell you before we share your information for any other reason.

Where your information is stored

Alurei operates across the Gulf, Singapore and the United States. Some of our service providers store or process data outside the country you’re in, including in the UAE, the European Union and the United States. When we transfer personal data outside the country it was collected in, we do so in line with the data protection law that applies to you, including UAE data protection law for partners in the UAE. That means using providers in countries with adequate protection, or contract terms that protect your information.

How long we keep it

  • Partner accounts: for as long as you’re a partner. Your listing comes off the app within 30 days after the partnership ends.
  • Redemption, statement and payment records: for as long as tax and commercial law requires, usually 5 to 7 years.
  • Businesses we contacted that didn’t become partners: 12 months after our last contact, or sooner if you ask us to delete them.
  • Messages and support history: up to 2 years after the partnership ends.

After that, we delete the information or make it anonymous.

How we protect it

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and secure hosting. Only the people who need partner information for their work can see it. Please keep your portal login private, and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.

Your rights

Under UAE data protection law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), and the laws of other countries where we operate, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • correct anything that’s wrong or out of date
  • delete your information, where we no longer need it
  • limit or stop how we use it, including for outreach and marketing
  • send your information to you or another provider in a usable format
  • withdraw your consent, where we rely on it

To make a request, email us. We’ll reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first. Some information we must keep by law, such as payment records, and we’ll explain if that applies.

If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled your request, you can complain to the UAE Data Office, or to the data protection authority in your country.
