1About these terms

These terms are an agreement between your business (“you”, “the partner”) and The Seasons (FZE), Block C, Sharjah Free Zone, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, which operates Alurei (“Alurei”, “we”, “us”).

You accept them by ticking the box when you sign up as a partner, or by keeping your listing live after we send you an updated version. The person who accepts them confirms they are allowed to do so on behalf of the business.

These terms, your partner schedule (the offer details you give us at sign-up or in the partner portal) and our Partner Privacy Policy together make up the whole agreement between us. If they conflict, your partner schedule comes first, then these terms.

2Becoming a partner

  • You must hold a valid trade licence or business registration for what you do, in the country where you operate, with any permits your activity needs (for example, for working with children).
  • The information you give us must be accurate and kept up to date.
  • There is no fee to be an Alurei partner, and we don’t take commission on bookings or sales you make with Alurei families.
  • We decide which partners to list. We may decline an application, or remove a listing, if it doesn’t fit Alurei’s standards or audience. We’ll tell you why where we can.

3Your listing and offers

What you give us. Your business name, logo, photos, description, addresses, opening hours, classes or services, and your Alurei offer. You confirm you have the right to use everything you send us, including photos. We may edit text for length and format, but not change your offer without asking you.

Keeping it current. You can update your listing and offer in the partner portal at any time. Changes to an offer apply from the day they’re published, and don’t affect bookings already made.

Activity partners (classes, play centres, camps, venues) agree to at least one of the following, as agreed with the Alurei team and recorded in your partner schedule:

  • A complimentary class or session for each child of an Alurei member family, once every 60 or 90 days (you choose which when you sign up)
  • An Alurei price on your courses, packages or sessions, which you set
  • An extra of your choice, such as a drink for the nanny who brings the child

You may limit complimentary classes to off-peak times if your schedule says so.

Reward partners (shops, cafés, salons and other businesses that welcome Alurei nannies and families) agree to:

  • honour the Alurei offer recorded in your partner schedule, as it appears in the app
  • accept Alurei vouchers or rewards, if your schedule includes them

All partners agree to treat Alurei families and nannies as you would any other customer, and to tell us if an offer is no longer available, so we can take it down.

4Families, nannies and bookings

Alurei shows your listing and offers to families and their nannies. Families and nannies book and pay you directly, usually by WhatsApp or at your venue. Alurei is not a party to that booking.

This means:

  • The family or nanny is your customer. Your own terms, prices, cancellation rules and safety procedures apply to the service you provide.
  • You are responsible for the safety and quality of your activities, venue and staff, including the checks, qualifications, supervision and insurance your activity requires. Alurei does not inspect venues or vet staff.
  • Alurei is not responsible for any booking, payment, refund, injury, loss or dispute between you and a family or nanny.
  • If a family raises a complaint about you with us, we’ll pass it to you and may ask you to respond. Serious or repeated complaints may lead us to pause or remove your listing.

We make no promise about how many families will see your listing, book with you or redeem offers.

5Vouchers and rewards

Some partnerships include accepting vouchers or rewards that nannies earn in the Alurei app. If yours does:

  • How redemption works, the value of each redemption, and how and when Alurei settles with you are set out in your partner schedule.
  • Your staff must check each voucher or reward as instructed before providing the goods or service.
  • Vouchers and rewards have no cash value and can’t be exchanged for cash.
  • If we suspect misuse, we may hold the affected redemptions while we look into it, and we’ll let you know.

6Brand and publicity

  • You allow us to show your business name, logo, photos and offer in the Alurei app, on alurei.app, in our emails and on our social media, to tell families and nannies about you.
  • You may say that you’re an Alurei partner and use the Alurei name and logo for that purpose, following the brand guidelines we give you. We’d love you to share the partnership with your own customers.
  • Neither of us may suggest the other endorses our products or services beyond this partnership, or use the other’s brand in a way that could mislead.
  • Each of us keeps ownership of our own brand, content and intellectual property. The Alurei app, its content library and its rewards programme belong to Alurei.
  • When the partnership ends, both of us stop using the other’s brand within 30 days, except for content already printed or posted.

7Data and privacy

  • We handle information about you and your staff as described in our Partner Privacy Policy.
  • When a family or nanny books with you, the details they share (such as a name, phone number or a child’s age) are given to you so you can provide the service. Use them only for that booking and for your own normal customer records. Don’t add them to marketing lists or share them with anyone else without the person’s clear consent.
  • Each of us is responsible for complying with data protection law that applies to us, including UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 where it applies.
  • Keep any Alurei login details, voucher codes and partner materials confidential, and only share them with staff who need them.
  • Tell us straight away if you become aware of a data breach involving Alurei families, nannies or systems.

8Ending the partnership

  • Either of us can end the partnership at any time by giving 14 days’ notice by email. No reason is needed.
  • We may pause or remove your listing straight away if you break these terms, if we receive a serious complaint about safety or conduct, if your licence lapses, or if we reasonably believe the partnership could harm Alurei families, nannies or our reputation. We’ll tell you what the issue is and, where it can be fixed, give you a chance to fix it.
  • Bookings, classes and redemptions already made before the end date must still be honoured.
  • Sections 6 (brand), 7 (data), 9 (liability) and 10 (law) continue to apply after the partnership ends.

9Responsibility and liability

  • You are responsible for the services you provide to families and nannies, for your staff and venue, and for complying with the laws, licences and permits that apply to your business. That includes UAE consumer protection law in your dealings with families, and any permit your promotions or offers need.
  • Alurei is responsible for running the app and the partner portal with reasonable care. We don’t promise they’ll always be available or error-free.
  • Neither of us is liable to the other for loss of profit, loss of business or indirect losses arising from this partnership.
  • Except for the matters below, each party’s total liability to the other under this agreement is limited to AED 10,000 or the total amount paid between us in the 12 months before the claim, whichever is higher.
  • Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability for fraud, gross negligence, death or personal injury, or anything else that UAE law doesn’t allow to be excluded or limited.

10Changes, law and general terms

Changes. We may update these terms from time to time. We’ll email you at least 14 days before a change takes effect. If you don’t agree, you can end the partnership before that date. Keeping your listing live after the date means you accept the new terms.

Electronic acceptance. Accepting these terms online has the same effect as signing them, under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 46 of 2021 on Electronic Transactions and Trust Services.

Tax. Any amounts payable between us are exclusive of VAT unless stated. Where VAT applies, a valid tax invoice must be issued.

Independent businesses. We are independent businesses. Nothing here creates a partnership in the legal sense, a joint venture, an agency or an employment relationship.

Notices. Formal notices go by email: to us at hello@alurei.app, and to you at the email address on your partner account.

Transfer. You may not transfer this agreement to another business without our written consent. We may transfer it to a company that takes over Alurei, and we’ll tell you if we do.

Governing law and courts. These terms are governed by the federal laws of the United Arab Emirates as applied in the Emirate of Sharjah. The courts of the Emirate of Sharjah have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute, after we’ve both made a genuine attempt to settle it between us within 30 days. If you’re based outside the UAE, a country addendum may apply to you, and we’ll tell you at sign-up.

Language. These terms are published in English and Arabic. We’ve made the two versions match. If they differ, the Arabic version applies.

If part of these terms is unenforceable, the rest still applies.
