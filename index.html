Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Email hello@alurei.app.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no fee to be an Alurei partner, and no commission on bookings or sales you make with Alurei families. In return, you offer a complimentary class, an Alurei price or a drink for the nanny, whichever you choose.

Who can join?

Classes, activity centres, play areas, cafés and other family venues with a valid trade licence and any permits needed for working with children. We review each application to make sure it's right for Alurei families.

Who will see my listing?

Families with a nanny, for the ages and times of day you choose. You appear inside the child's Daily Flow when your class or venue fits their day.

How do families use the complimentary class?

The family or nanny books with you directly, usually by WhatsApp or at your venue, and the nanny shows the offer in the Alurei app when they arrive. Your own booking and cancellation rules apply, and Alurei isn't part of the booking or payment. We'll walk you through the simple steps when we set up your listing.

Can I change my offer?

Yes. You can update your offer in the partner portal at any time. Changes apply from when they're published and don't affect bookings already made. To end the partnership, just give us 14 days' notice by email.

What happens after I sign up?

We'll review your details and contact you to confirm your offer. Once your listing is approved, it goes live in families' Daily Flow.

How will Alurei use my brand?

We show your name, logo, photos and offer in the app, on alurei.app, in our emails and on social media. You're welcome to say you're an Alurei partner. Details families share with you are for their booking only, not for marketing lists.
