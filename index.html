Is it really free?

Yes. There’s no fee to be an Alurei partner, and no commission on bookings or sales you make with Alurei families. In return, you choose one, two or all three: a complimentary class, an Alurei price and a drink for the nanny.

How do families use the complimentary class?

One class per child, every 60 or 90 days (you choose). For a play area, one complimentary hour on the same cycle. The family or nanny books with you directly, usually by WhatsApp or at your venue. At your front desk, the nanny taps “Use complimentary class” in the Alurei app and shows you the confirmation: the child’s first name, your venue, the date and time, and the drink for the nanny if you offer one. There are no codes or vouchers and nothing for you to record: the app keeps track, and each month you get a report of complimentary classes used, discount claims and families who came back. Your own booking and cancellation rules apply, and Alurei isn’t part of the booking or payment.

How do families use the Alurei price?

When paying for a class or package, the nanny or parent taps “Use Alurei price” in the app and shows you the confirmation with your discount, and you apply it. Families can use it any time they pay, including on the same day as the complimentary class.

Who will see my listing?

Families with a nanny, for the ages and times of day you choose. You appear inside the child’s Daily Flow when your class or venue fits their day.

What happens after I sign up?

We’ll review your details and contact you within two working days to confirm your offer. Once your listing is approved, it goes live in families’ Daily Flow.

Can I change my offer?

Yes. You can change or pause your offer at any time in the partner portal. Changes apply from when they’re published and don’t affect bookings already made. To end the partnership, just give us 14 days’ notice by email.

Who can join?

Nurseries and early learning, kids’ classes and activities, soft play and party venues, family cafés and restaurants, children’s health and wellness, mum and family wellness, baby and kids’ shops, family travel and staycations, and home and family services. You need a valid trade licence and any permits needed for working with children, and we review each application to make sure it’s right for Alurei families.

My venue is in Abu Dhabi. Can I join?

Alurei is starting with families across Dubai, and Abu Dhabi is coming soon. Abu Dhabi venues are welcome to get in touch now: book a 10-minute call or leave your WhatsApp number above.

How will Alurei use my brand?

We show your name, logo, photos and offer in the app, on alurei.app, in our emails and on social media. You’re welcome to say you’re an Alurei partner. Details families share with you are for their booking only, not for marketing lists.
